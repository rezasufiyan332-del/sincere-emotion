## 1. Discover Your Style in 3 Minutes

### Quick Quiz (Pick the column that feels most like you)

| Situation | **Column A** | **Column B** | **Column C** |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|--------------|
| **Partner doesn't text back 2 hrs** | Panic. "Are they leaving?" Check phone obsessively | "Good, space." Don't notice / don't care | "They're busy. I'll hear later." Continue your day |
| **Conflict arises** | Pursue. "Talk to me NOW." Escalate to get response | Withdraw. "I'm fine." Shut down / leave | Pause. "I'm upset. Let's talk in 20 min." Return to resolve |
| **Partner shares vulnerable feeling** | Relate intensely. Make it about you. Over-comfort | Uncomfortable. Intellectualize. "Have you tried...?" | Listen. "That sounds hard. Tell me more." |
| **You need something** | Hint. Hope they guess. Resent when they don't | Don't ask. "I don't need anything." Resent silently | Ask directly. "I need ___. Can you ___?" |
| **Relationship deepens** | "Do they really love me?" Test. Seek reassurance | "This is dangerous." Find flaws. Create distance | "This is good." Share vulnerability. Build trust |
| **Breakup / distance** | Protest. Chase. Beg. Collapse. | Relief. Numb. Move on fast. Avoid grief. | Grieve. Process. Learn. Stay open to love. |

### Your Result

**Mostly Column A → ANXIOUS ATTACHMENT**  
*Core fear: Abandonment. Core need: Reassurance.*

**Mostly Column B → AVOIDANT ATTACHMENT**  
*Core fear: Engulfment. Core need: Autonomy.*

**Mostly Column C → SECURE ATTACHMENT**  
*Core belief: "I'm worthy. Others are trustworthy. We can handle this."*

**Mix of A & B → FEARFUL-AVOIDANT (Disorganized)**  
*Core conflict: "Come close / Go away." Needs trauma-informed support.*

## 3. Your Style's Superpower & Shadow

### Anxious Attachment

| **Superpower** | **Shadow** |
|----------------|------------|
| Deep empathy & attunement | Emotional flooding |
| Relentless commitment | Self-abandonment |
| Rich emotional vocabulary | Protest behaviors |
| Values connection deeply | Catastrophizing |
| Incredible capacity to love | Loses self in other |

**Growth Edge:** Self-regulation → Self-trust → Secure autonomy

---

### Avoidant Attachment

| **Superpower** | **Shadow** |
|----------------|------------|
| Strong autonomy & boundaries | Emotional suppression |
| Calm under pressure | Intimacy avoidance |
| Clear thinking in chaos | Deactivation = disconnection |
| Low drama tolerance | Misses connection cues |
| Resilient independence | Equates closeness with danger |

**Growth Edge:** Vulnerability → Interdependence → Secure intimacy

---

### Secure Attachment

| **Superpower** | **Growth Area** |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Self-worth independent of other | Can become complacent |
| Regulates under stress | May not recognize partner's insecurity |
| Direct communication | Can over-function for insecure partner |
| Repairs quickly & genuinely | May need to learn partner's language |
| Chooses well, loves deeply | Continuous practice, not destination |

**Growth Edge:** Deepening mastery → Helping others secure → Legacy

## 4. Immediate Regulation Tools

### For ANXIOUS Activation (Hyperarousal)

**When panic hits (chest tight, thoughts racing, urge to text):**

| Tool | How | Time |
|------|-----|------|
| **Cold Water Face** | Splash 30 sec or ice pack on neck | 30 sec |
| **4-7-8 Breath** | Inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8 × 4 | 2 min |
| **Voo Sound** | Deep "voooo" on exhale × 5 | 1 min |
| **5-4-3-2-1 Ground** | 5 see, 4 touch, 3 hear, 2 smell, 1 taste | 2 min |
| **Wall Push** | Push wall 10 sec × 3 (complete fight) | 1 min |
| **Self-Touch** | Hand on heart: "I'm here. You're safe." | 1 min |

**Mantra:** *"This is activation, not reality. I can regulate. I am safe."*

---

### For AVOIDANT Shutdown (Hypoarousal)

**When numb/frozen (flat, "fine," urge to disappear):**

| Tool | How | Time |
|------|-----|------|
| **Micro-Movement** | Wiggle toes → roll shoulders → stand up | 30 sec |
| **Sensory Shock** | Cold water / strong mint / rough texture | 30 sec |
| **Jump/Shake** | 20 jumping jacks or full body shake | 1 min |
| **Hum/Sigh** | Audible exhale "haaa" × 5 | 30 sec |
| **Orient** | Name 3 things each: see, hear, feel | 1 min |
| **Voice** | Speak aloud: "I'm here. I'm back." | 30 sec |

**Mantra:** *"This is protection, not truth. I can feel. I am safe."*

---

### For EVERYONE: The 90-Second Rule

**Emotions = chemical surges lasting ~90 seconds.** After that, you're re-stimulating with thoughts.

**Practice:**
1. Trigger hits
2. Set mental timer: 90 seconds
3. **Pure sensation only:** "Heat in chest. Tight throat. Racing heart."
4. **No story. No "why." Just sensation.**
5. After 90s → Choose response from regulation
